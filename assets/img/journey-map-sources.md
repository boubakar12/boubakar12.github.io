# Journey globe assets

## journey-earth.jpg

Source: Natural Earth I, 1:50m, shaded relief and water, version 3.2.0.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-natural-earth-1/
https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/50m_raster/NE1_50M_SR_W.zip

The 10800 × 5400 RGB GeoTIFF was resized to a 4096 × 2048 JPEG (quality 86)
for use as a local equirectangular globe texture. It shows land cover and
shaded relief; it is not a street-level imagery service.

## journey-land.svg

Source: Natural Earth 1:110m land outlines.
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

The SVG uses Mercator coordinates for the local map fallback.

Natural Earth raster and vector data are public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
