I’m studying Electrical & Computer Engineering at Cornell University (B.S. May 2028). I work across digital hardware, embedded software, robotics, and applied AI — from RISC-V datapaths and real-time scheduling to sensor interfaces and on-device inference.

I like engineering that has to meet a physical constraint: a timing budget, a noisy sensor, a limited power supply, or a small memory footprint. I’m interested in how decisions at one layer affect the rest of a system, and I use simulation, instrumentation, and field testing to close that loop.

At ADAS SAFE, I worked on automotive perception-hardware validation. At Engineers Without Borders, I led rover electronics and robotics integration. I then built real-time data infrastructure as a Software Engineer Intern at Simulacrum from January to June 2026. Next, I’m joining PNC as a Technology Intern in summer 2027.

My recent projects include a five-stage RISC-V processor, an ARM real-time scheduler, a bidirectional occupancy monitor, and forecasting research. WholeStory Health brings my interests in language and engineering together. I speak English, French, Arabic, and Fulah.
