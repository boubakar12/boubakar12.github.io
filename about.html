I'm a sophomore at Cornell University studying Electrical & Computer Engineering (B.S. May 2028), with a minor in Business and Computer Science. I work at the intersection of hardware and software — from bare-metal firmware and FPGA design to edge ML systems that run fully offline in the field.

My work is shaped by a simple observation: the most critical engineering problems — healthcare in low-resource settings, crop disease detection in Sub-Saharan Africa, safety-critical automotive systems — exist in places where reliability under constraints matters more than cloud scale. I build for those environments.

I'm currently interning at Simulacrum in New York, building embedded data platforms. I lead the hardware team at Engineers Without Borders, deploying a rover-drone system for crop analytics. And I'm building WholeStory Health — a multilingual clinical NLP platform that runs fully offline on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano hardware, cutting physician documentation time in under-resourced clinics.

I was selected nationally for Beta University's Silicon Valley pre-accelerator and hold 4 competitive national merit scholarships (top ~2% of applicants each). Fluent in English, French, Arabic, and Fulah.
